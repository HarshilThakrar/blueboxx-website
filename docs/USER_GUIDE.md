# User Guide

Complete manual for all 6 end-user roles on the BlueBoxx DA platform.

---

## 1. Student

### Workflow: Register → Learn → Get Certified

**Step 1: Register**
1. Go to `/signup` and select the **Student** role.
2. Fill in your name, email, and password.
3. Verify your email via the OTP sent to your inbox.

**Step 2: Browse & Enroll**
1. Navigate to `/courses` to browse available programs.
2. Use the search bar and category filters to narrow down.
3. Click on a course card to view details, curriculum, and instructor info.
4. Click **Enroll Now** → You will be redirected to the checkout page.

**Step 3: Payment**
1. Review your cart on `/checkout`.
2. Click **Pay Now** — The Razorpay payment gateway will open.
3. On success, you will be automatically redirected to `/payment-success` and enrollment is confirmed.

**Step 4: Learn & Get Certified**
1. Access your enrolled courses from the **Student Dashboard** → **My Courses**.
2. Watch video lessons, complete quizzes, and submit assignments.
3. Upon completing all requirements, your **certificate is generated automatically**.
4. Download your certificate from the **Certificates** section.

---

## 2. Job Seeker

### Workflow: Upload Resume → Apply → Track Status

1. Complete your profile at `/profile` including skills, experience, and education.
2. Upload your resume (PDF, max 5MB) from the **Profile** section.
3. Browse jobs at `/jobs` using filters (Location, Job Type, Salary Range).
4. Click **Apply Now** on a job card → your saved resume is submitted instantly.
5. Track application status under **Dashboard → My Applications**.

---

## 3. Intern

### Workflow: Apply → Upload Resume → Track

1. Browse internships at `/internships` with category and stipend filters.
2. Apply directly from the internship detail page.
3. Track application progress (Applied → Shortlisted → Offered) in your **Intern Dashboard**.

---

## 4. Expert (Mentor)

### Workflow: Complete Profile → Accept Bookings → Conduct Sessions

1. Complete your Expert Profile (specializations, hourly rate, availability schedule).
2. Students will book sessions via `/book-session/{expert_id}`.
3. You will receive a **booking notification** via email and in-app notification.
4. Conduct the session and the payment is automatically released post-session.

---

## 5. Company

### Workflow: Register → Get Approved → Post Jobs → Hire

1. Register at `/signup` selecting the **Company** role.
2. Await Admin approval (typically 24 hours).
3. Complete your Company Profile after approval.
4. Post Jobs and Internships from your **Company Dashboard**.
5. Shortlisted applicants' resumes can be **downloaded directly** from the applicant table.
6. Schedule interviews from the applications panel and send automated interview notifications.

---

## 6. College

### Workflow: Register → Get Approved → Run Placement Drives

1. Register and await Admin approval.
2. Create a **Placement Drive** from your dashboard with participating company details and drive schedule.
3. Students from your institution can register for drives.
4. Export **Placement Reports** as CSV for institutional records.
